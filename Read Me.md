\# Heavy Supplier, Inventory \& Warehouse Analytics





\## Project Overview

This project focuses on building a comprehensive Supply Chain \& Retail Analytics Solution using "Power BI" for CadetX Free Virtual Internship Program. The dashboard provides valuable business insights into Sales Performance, Procurement (Supplier Analytics), and Warehouse operations using a dataset of 25 Billion in revenue.





\## Star Schema

A professional "Data Model" was designed using Relationships to optimize performance.

* Dimension Tables:  Branches, Customers, Inventory Master, Products, Suppliers.
* Fact Tables: Invoices, Payments, Purchase Orders Header/Lines, Sales Orders Header/Lines, Stock Ledger.





\## Key Dashboards \& Insights Built

1. Sales Performance Dashboard: Displays Total Revenue, Total Sales Orders, Top 5 Products, and Sales by Branch. Included interactive slicers for Payment Terms.
2. Procurement & Supplier Analysis: Analyzed Total Spend, Purchase Orders Count, Top Suppliers by Spend, and Purchase distributions across Branches.
3. Warehouse Operations: Monitored Total Warehouse Capacity, Stock Value by Category, and analyzes Stock Movements.





\## DAX Functions Used:

* 'DISTINCTCOUNT': Used to calculate unique customers and orders without duplicates.
* 'SUMX': Used for row-by-row calculation of Quantity multiplied by Unit Cost to find accurate Revenue and Spend.





\## Tools Used

* Power BI Desktop: Power Query to column cleaning (separating "sqft" text from capacity numbers). Data Modeling, DAX calculations, and Interactive Visualizations.
